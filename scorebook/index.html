Score the set

Big buttons between shots.

Best-of-three sets, rack by rack. Solids or stripes, who's on the break next time, and the wild moments: 8 on the break, ran the table, golden break.

Shared, live

Every rack, both phones.

Invite your opponent and the book lives on both iPhones through iCloud. Every rack shows up on the other phone in seconds, with the score on the Lock Screen during a set and widgets on the Home Screen.

Your halls

Where you play, and who owns it.

Save the halls you play at, with photos, notes and where they are. A real table behind your score, in the cloth you pick. All-time standings, streaks, and who owns which hall.
