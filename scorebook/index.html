Pool Position: Scorebook

Pool scorebook
for rivals.

A scorebook for two players who play 8-ball and 9-ball together. Score every set rack by rack, and keep the record that settles the argument.

Coming soon to the App StoreiPhone and iPad

Score the set

Best-of-three sets, rack by rack, with big buttons you can hit between shots. Solids or stripes, who's on the break next time, and the wild moments: 8 on the break, ran the table, golden break.

Shared, live

Invite your opponent and the book lives on both iPhones through iCloud. Every rack shows up on the other phone in seconds, with the score on the Lock Screen during a set and widgets on the Home Screen.

The record

All-time standings, including the record from before you started. Streaks, solids against stripes, and who owns which hall.

Your halls

Save the halls you play at, with photos, notes and where they are. A real table behind your score, in the cloth you pick. Made for iPhone Duo, too.
