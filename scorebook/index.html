Pool Position: Scorebook

Pool scorebook
for rivals.

For two players who play 8-ball and 9-ball together. Score every set ball by ball, and keep the record that settles the argument.

Coming soon to the App StoreiPhone and iPad

Score the set

Tap each ball as it drops.

Tap the ball that went in, and Turn over when they miss. At 8-ball the first legal ball sets solids and stripes, and the 8 decides the rack. 9-ball counts points.

A foul passes the turn, and the top line tells you who breaks the next rack.

Your format

Best of 3, or your league's race.

Play a best of 1, 3, 5 or 7. Or play it league style: 9-ball on points, 8-ball as a race in racks, each player with their own number.

Shared, live

Every ball, both phones.

Invite your opponent and the book lives on both iPhones through iCloud. Each tap shows up on the other phone in seconds. The score sits on the Lock Screen during a set, and widgets keep it on the Home Screen.

Your halls

Pick the room behind the score.

Palmer's, the Home Lounge, Pub 85 or Classic: choose the scene from the row on the Table.

Save the halls you play at, with photos, notes and where they are.
