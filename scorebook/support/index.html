Support

For iPhone and iPad

Getting started

Open the app and tap Start a book. Enter your name and your opponent's, pick cars, and start scoring. To share the book, tap Invite on the Table (or in Setup) and send the link. Your opponent taps it on their iPhone, opens Pool Position, and picks which player they are.

Common questions

The app says iCloud is needed.
Books are stored in iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in Settings, and check iCloud Drive is on.
My opponent's phone isn't updating.
Both phones need to be online and signed in to iCloud. Updates usually arrive in a few seconds; opening the app fetches the latest.
Can I have books with different people?
Yes. Tap the book's name at the top of the Table to start a New book or switch to another one.
The jukebox is quiet.
Paste an Apple Music playlist link in Setup › Jukebox. Full songs need an Apple Music subscription.
How do I delete my data?
Setup › Delete this book (or Leave this book). See the privacy policy.
