Score the set

Tap each ball as it drops.

Tap the ball that went in, and Turn over when they miss. At 8-ball the first legal ball sets solids and stripes, and the 8 decides the rack. 9-ball by points counts a point a ball, and two for the 9.

A foul passes the turn, and the top line tells you who breaks the next rack.

Your format

Best of 3, or your league's race.

Play a best of 1, 3, 5 or 7. Or play it league style: 9-ball on points, 8-ball as a race in racks, each player with their own number. League, Tournament or Bar rules.

Shared, live

Every ball, both phones.

Invite your opponent and the book lives on both iPhones through iCloud. Each tap shows up on the other phone in seconds. The score sits on the Lock Screen during a set, and widgets keep it on the Home Screen.

Made for iPhone Duo

Read the score from across the room.

Open the Duo or stand it on the rail like a laptop: the score fills the top half, big enough to read from your chair, and every ball to tap sits on the bottom half.
