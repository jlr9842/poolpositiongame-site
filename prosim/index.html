Pool Position: ProSim

Step up to
the table.

8-ball and 9-ball with physics measured against the real thing, in rooms that feel like somewhere you'd play.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Quick to the table

One tap and you're playing.

Your first rack is three balls and three pockets, one tip at a time. After that the title is one tap from a rack: against the House, with someone, or practice.

Tonight's rack is one seeded rack a night, the same for everyone, with a count of the nights you played this week.

The stroke is the skill

Aim, then really stroke it.

Line up from behind the cue ball, set your tip for spin, then pull back and push through: your stroke sets the pace.

Someone to play

The regulars are waiting.

Take on the House at three levels, each with its own regulars and their own style. Practice alone against the ghost, in 8-ball or 9-ball.

Physics first

Balls that behave like balls.

Ball collisions, cushions, cloth and spin are modeled on published research and measurements of real tables. Hit it hard and the balls behave like balls.

Chalk matters too: the tip wears with every shot, and forgetting to chalk can cost you a miscue.

Play together

Couch pool, two ways.

Put the table on the Apple TV and use your iPhone as the cue. Or skip the TV: two iPhones, one table.

Apple TV

The table fills the room.

Join from your iPhone with the code on the TV, pass one phone around, or play with just the Siri Remote or a game controller. Set up on the phone and rack 'em.

Two phones, one table

Same room, no TV needed.

Hold the two iPhones together, or host a table and your friend joins with the code on their iPhone. Each of you plays your own shots on your own phone, and both phones show every one.

Made for iPhone Duo

Fold it, open it, the table follows.

Folded, the controls sit at your thumbs. Open flat, the whole table. As a book, the shot on one page and the plan on the other. As a laptop, the table up top and the controls below.

Rooms worth playing in

A pool room and a home lounge.

Play under the three lamps of a neighborhood pool room with its regulars, or at the home lounge's table. Each room has its own cloth, light and sound.

iPad

A bigger table.

The same simulator on iPad, with room to see the whole rack.
