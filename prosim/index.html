Pool Position: ProSim

Step up to
the table.

A pool simulator made for the big screen. 8-ball and 9-ball on physics measured from real tables, in rooms worth playing in.

Coming to Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

Apple TV first

The table fills the room.

Home opens over the table you played last. Continue picks up where you left off: your next league night, a regular, a lesson. Or play on your own, or together.

Play with the Siri Remote, a game controller, or your iPhone as the cue.

Physics first

Balls that behave like balls.

Collisions, cushions, cloth and spin come from published research and measurements of real tables.

Chalk wears down as you play. Forget it and you can miscue.

League nights

Climb the levels.

Each room runs a league night. Win and your level moves up. When it doesn't, ProSim shows you what would help, and which regular can teach it.

The regulars

Everyone in the room has a game.

Every room has its regulars, from easy to hard, each with their own habits and their own cue. Play them for fun, take their lesson, then play them for the cue.

Lessons

Learn it from the regulars.

Fran's position lesson: pot the 1 and leave the cue ball in the ring. Five layouts on the same physics as every rack, and after each try, how close you came and what to change.

iPhone and iPad

The same game in your hand.

The phone has the same Home and the same league. Aim from behind the cue ball and set your spin. Then pull back and push forward on the pad: the speed of your push is the speed of the shot.

After every league night

See where you stand.

Your level, the form behind it, and the lesson that would help most, picked from your own shots.

Play together

Who's in?

Two to four players at one table. Fill the seats and the count picks the game: with four, it's doubles. Pass one phone round, use two phones, or put the table on the Apple TV.

Three rooms

Pick a room, pick a table.

Palmer's is a neighborhood pool room. The Home Lounge is a table at home. Pub 85 is a big hall with two tables, a blue one and a red one. Every room has its own cloth, light and sound.
