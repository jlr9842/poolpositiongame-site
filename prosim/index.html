Pool Position: ProSim

Step up to
the table.

8-ball and 9-ball on physics measured from real tables. Three rooms, each with its own feel.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Quick to the table

Rack 'em in one tap.

Your first rack is three balls and three pockets, with a pointer or two along the way. After that, one tap on the title gets you a rack: against the House, against a friend, or on your own.

The cue in your hands

Line it up. Pull back. Follow through.

Aim from behind the cue ball and set your spin. Then pull back and push forward on the pad. The speed of your push is the speed of the shot.

Someone to play

The regulars are waiting.

Take on the House at three levels. Each level has its own regulars, with their own habits. Or practice alone against the ghost, at 8-ball or 9-ball.

Physics first

Balls that behave like balls.

Collisions, cushions, cloth and spin come from published research and measurements of real tables.

Chalk wears down as you play. Forget it and you can miscue.

Three rooms

Pick a room, pick a table.

Palmer's is a neighborhood pool room with three lamps over the table. The Home Lounge is a table at home. Pub 85 is a big hall with two tables, a blue one and a red one. Every room has its own cloth, light and sound.

Play together

Couch pool, two ways.

Put the table on the Apple TV and use your iPhone as the cue. Or skip the TV: two iPhones, one table.

Apple TV

The table fills the room.

Join from your iPhone with the code on the TV, pass one phone around, or play with just the Siri Remote or a game controller.

Two phones, one table

Same room, no TV.

Hold the two iPhones together, or host a table and your friend joins with the code. You each shoot on your own phone, and both phones show every shot.

Made for iPhone Duo

Fold it, open it, the table follows.

Folded, the controls sit under your thumbs. Open flat, you get the whole table. As a book, the shot on one page and the plan on the other. As a laptop, the table up top and the controls below.
