Pool Position: ProSim

Step up to
the table.

8-ball and 9-ball with physics measured against the real thing, in rooms that feel like somewhere you'd play.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Aim, then really stroke it

Line up from behind the cue ball, set your tip for spin, then pull back and push through: your stroke sets the pace.

Walk round the table to read the shot, crouch to the rail, and step back in without losing your aim.

Physics first

Ball collisions, cushions, cloth and spin are modelled on published research and measurements of real tables. Hit it hard and the balls behave like balls.

Chalk matters too: the tip wears with every shot, and forgetting to chalk can cost you a miscue.

Big screen, phone in hand

On Apple TV the table fills the room. Join with your iPhone as the cue, or play with just the Siri Remote or a game controller.

Or put two iPhones side by side and play at one table.

Someone to play

Play a friend, or take on the regulars at three levels. Practise alone against the ghost, in 8-ball or 9-ball.
