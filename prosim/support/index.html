Support

ProSim is coming to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. These answers are for testers today and players later.

Testing the beta

Thanks for testing ProSim before it's out. Here's how to tell us what you find.

Send feedback from the app
Open You › Send feedback. It writes an email to us with your app version, build and device filled in, so add what happened and what you expected. Screenshots help.
Send feedback through TestFlight
Take a screenshot while you play and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app, choose ProSim and tap Send Beta Feedback. If ProSim crashes, TestFlight asks whether to send the crash report: please say yes.
What we'd love to hear
Does the cue ball do what you expect? Does the break feel right? After a league match, did your level move the way you'd expect? Anything that crashed, froze or made your phone hot, and where it happened.
What we collect
Nothing. ProSim has no analytics and no tracking. An email you send is only what you write, and a crash report goes through Apple's TestFlight only if you agree.

Common questions

How do I play on my Apple TV with my iPhone?
Open ProSim on the Apple TV and it shows a join code. Open ProSim on your iPhone, choose to play on the Apple TV and enter the code. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi, and the iPhone needs local network access (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).
Can I play on the Apple TV without a phone?
Yes. Press Select on the Siri Remote, or A on a game controller, to play with just the remote or controller.
My records aren't on my other device.
Your profile and records sync through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and check iCloud is on for ProSim.
