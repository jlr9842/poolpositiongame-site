Support

ProSim is coming to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. These answers are for testers today and players later.

Common questions

How do I play on my Apple TV with my iPhone?
Open ProSim on the Apple TV and it shows a join code. Open ProSim on your iPhone, choose to play on the Apple TV and enter the code. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi, and the iPhone needs local network access (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).
Can I play on the Apple TV without a phone?
Yes. Press Select on the Siri Remote, or A on a game controller, to play with just the remote or controller.
My records aren't on my other device.
Your profile and records sync through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and check iCloud is on for ProSim.
